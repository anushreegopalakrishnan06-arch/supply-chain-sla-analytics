## 🖥️ Slide 2: The Core Business Challenge
* **The Problem:** 
  * In global fulfillment, delivery delays trigger immediate customer churn and costly concession refunds.
  * Operations leadership lacked visibility into whether delays originate inside the warehouse (seller) or during road transit (carrier).
* **Key Metrics Tracked:**
  * **PDD Adherence Rate** (Promised Delivery Date)
  * **Network DPMO** (Defects Per Million Opportunities)
  * **Customer Satisfaction Decay** (1-5 Star Ratings)
  * **Concession Liability Exposure** ($ Loss)

---

## 🖥️ Slide 3: Executive Diagnostic Findings (The Numbers)
* **Total Shipments Analyzed:** 99,441 Completed Orders
* **SLA Breach Rate:** **7.87%** (7,827 Overdue Deliveries)
* **Network DPMO:** **78,269** (Target: < 20,000 DPMO)
* **Average Overdue Delay:** **+5.4 Days** beyond customer commitment
* **Financial Exposure:** **$284,500** in estimated customer concession liabilities and shipping fee refunds

---

## 🖥️ Slide 4: Root-Cause Decomposition (Seller vs Carrier)
* **The Core Finding:** **Carrier Transit represents 82.4% of total delay time!**
* **Stage Comparison (Average Days):**
  * *On-Time Orders:* Seller Handover: **2.8 Days** | Carrier Transit: **8.0 Days** (Total: 11.2 Days)
  * *Breached Orders:* Seller Handover: **3.8 Days** | Carrier Transit: **27.1 Days** (Total: 31.4 Days)
* **Takeaway:** *"Warehouse packing is operating efficiently (+1 day variance); line-haul carrier freight transit is the primary failure point (+19.1 days variance)."*

---

## 🖥️ Slide 5: The Cost of Delay (Customer Review Decay)
* **Review Score Collapse:**
  * On-Time Deliveries: **4.32 / 5.00 Average Score** (81.4% 5-Star Reviews)
  * 1–3 Days Late: **2.57 / 5.00 Average Score** (35% 1-Star Reviews)
  * > 7 Days Late: **1.62 / 5.00 Average Score** (68.2% 1-Star Reviews)
* **Insight:** Customer goodwill deteriorates rapidly after the first 24 hours of delay; proactive intervention must happen *within Day 1 of breach*.

---

## 🖥️ Slide 6: Geographic Bottleneck Heatmap
* **Worst Performing Corridors:**
  * Dispatches from Southeast Hubs (SP, RJ) to Northeast consumer regions (AL, MA, AM) exceed **22% SLA breach rates**.
* **Driver:** Inadequate cross-dock facilities and reliance on single-mode road freight over 2,500+ km distances.

---

## 🖥️ Slide 7: Strategic Recommendations & ROI
1. **Dynamic SLA Buffering:** Add algorithmic +3 day buffer to Southeast-to-Northeast transit corridors (immediately recovers customer expectation on 2,800 orders/yr).
2. **Carrier SLA Penalty Tiering:** Implement contractual 15%/day chargebacks on transit delays exceeding 5 days (recovers ~$62,000 annually).
3. **Proactive Pre-Breach Communication:** Trigger automated tracking alerts and proactive $5 goodwill credits when a parcel remains idle > 48h in transit hubs.
